04 — How I approach problems

Evidence first, then the smallest fix.

  1. Step 1

    Understand the problem

    Write down what is failing, for whom, and how often.

  2. Step 2

    Investigate the evidence

    Read logs, query plans and real data before forming a theory.

  3. Step 3

    Design a practical solution

    Choose the simplest design that meets the requirement.

  4. Step 4

    Implement and validate

    Change one thing at a time and measure the result again.

  5. Step 5

    Document lessons

    Record what changed and why, so the next person starts ahead.
